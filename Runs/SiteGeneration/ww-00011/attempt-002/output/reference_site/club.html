Our Story

A Club Built on Courts and Character

Since 1923, Courtside & Crown has shaped players and people in equal measure.

What began as a small grass-court club in the heart of the county has grown into one of the region's most respected tennis academies. Our walls carry the portraits of champions; our courts carry the footsteps of thousands of members whose lives were changed by the game.

Our History

One Hundred Years, One Unbroken Tradition

1923 — Founded

Courtside & Crown opens with four grass courts and thirty founding members on the Harrowfield estate.

1951 — First County Title

Our men's first team claims the county championship for the first time, cementing our competitive reputation.

1974 — Clubhouse Expansion

The iconic red-brick clubhouse is extended, adding a viewing terrace, lounge, and junior changing facilities.

1989 — Junior Academy Launched

A dedicated junior coaching pathway is introduced, producing three national-ranked juniors within a decade.

2003 — Indoor Courts Added

Two all-weather indoor courts ensure year-round play and open the academy to a wider community.

2023 — Centenary Season

Courtside & Crown celebrates 100 years with a centenary tournament, alumni weekend, and the launch of our scholarship programme.

Our Facilities

Courts Worthy of Champions

Eight courts, a storied clubhouse, and everything a serious player needs to thrive.

4 Grass Courts

Meticulously maintained to Wimbledon-standard specifications, open from May through September.

2 All-Weather Hard Courts

Available twelve months a year for training, club matches, and private hire.

2 Indoor Courts

Climate-controlled and floodlit, ensuring no session is ever cancelled by the British weather.

Heritage Clubhouse

A warm, members-only space featuring a lounge, bar, trophy room, and viewing terrace overlooking the grass courts.

Ball Machine Studio

A dedicated solo practice bay equipped with a professional ball machine for drilling at any time.

Fitness Suite

A compact on-site gym tailored for tennis-specific conditioning, used by our performance squad daily.
