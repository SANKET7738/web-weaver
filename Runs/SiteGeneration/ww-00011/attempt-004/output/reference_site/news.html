Featured Story

One Hundred Years of Courtside & Crown: A Century Remembered

From four grass courts on a country estate to a thriving academy of three thousand members — the story of how a club became a community.

When Colonel Edmund Harrowfield laid out the first four courts on his estate in the summer of 1923, he could not have imagined that a century later, thousands of players would call this place their sporting home. What began as a gentlemen's pastime became, over the decades, a school of champions, a refuge for beginners, and a gathering place for families across the county. The centenary season is not a celebration of the past alone — it is a declaration of intent. The next hundred years start now.
