Common Questions

Before You Write, We May Already Have Your Answer

Do I need to be an experienced player to join?

Not at all. We welcome complete beginners of all ages. Our adult beginner programme and junior starter sessions are designed for players with no prior experience.

Can I try the club before committing to membership?

Yes. We offer a complimentary trial session for all prospective members. Contact the office to arrange a suitable time.

How do I enter a squad or competitive team?

Competitive squad places are allocated following a brief assessment session with our head coach. Contact the coaching team to arrange a trial.

Is the club suitable for children with disabilities?

Courtside & Crown is committed to inclusive tennis. We offer adapted tennis sessions and our facilities are accessible. Please contact us to discuss your child's specific needs.

Can I hire a court without being a member?

Non-member court hire is available at weekday off-peak times, subject to availability. Contact the office for rates and booking.
