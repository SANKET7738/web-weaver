Premier Neighborhoods

The Addresses That Define the City

Financial District

The pulse of the city's commerce, with sleek high-rises, Michelin-starred dining, and unrivaled connectivity. Ideal for executives and urban professionals.

Avg. Price $3.2M
Property Types Penthouses, Condos

Harbor Heights

Sweeping waterfront views, yacht clubs, and a relaxed coastal atmosphere just minutes from the city center. A sanctuary for those who want space and prestige.

Avg. Price $5.8M
Property Types Estates, Villas

Westfield Park

Tree-lined streets, historic brownstones, and a walkable village core with boutique shops and acclaimed restaurants. Timeless, established, and deeply coveted.

Avg. Price $2.9M
Property Types Townhouses, Single-Family

Ridgecrest Hills

Gated estates on expansive lots with sweeping hilltop views. The city's most private and prestigious residential enclave, favored by executives and celebrities.

Avg. Price $9.4M
Property Types Manors, Estates

Midtown Arts

A vibrant cultural district where galleries, live music venues, and acclaimed chefs converge. Attracts creative professionals and design-forward buyers.

Avg. Price $1.8M
Property Types Lofts, Condos

Gallery Row

A curated enclave of boutique residences, world-class art institutions, and quiet, walkable streets. Refined, cosmopolitan, and consistently in demand.

Avg. Price $2.4M
Property Types Apartments, Condos
