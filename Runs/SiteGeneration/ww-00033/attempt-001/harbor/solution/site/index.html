Why Forma Intime

Three principles. No compromises.

Anatomical Construction

Each pattern is drafted against a library of 12 body profiles, not a single standard size. Seams follow the body's natural lines — never across pressure points.

Material Integrity

We source only certified, traceable fabrics: medical-grade modal, Tencel blends, and recycled nylon with verified colorfastness. What touches your skin is documented.

Longevity Protocol

Every garment is stress-tested for 200 wash cycles before release. We publish the results. Forma Intime pieces are designed to outlast fast fashion by years.
