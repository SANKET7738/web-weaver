Process Documentation — S/S Campaign
The Process

We started with fit sessions, not mood boards.

For the current campaign, we invited 40 women to our fit studio over six weeks — not models, but women who wear Forma Intime daily. Each session began with a body mapping consultation: posture, movement range, pressure-point documentation. The garments were adjusted in real time. What you see in the campaign is the result of those sessions — not a styled interpretation of them. The lighting is clinical. The framing is direct. The women are wearing pieces that were fitted to their specific anatomy. We believe that is the most honest thing a lingerie brand can show.
