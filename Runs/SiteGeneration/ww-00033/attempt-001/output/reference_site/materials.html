Our Material Brief

We started with a question dermatologists ask.

When Forma Intime was founded, the first question our design team asked wasn't "what looks beautiful?" — it was "what is safe to wear against the skin for 16 hours a day?"

That question led us to dermatology literature, textile toxicology studies, and conversations with fabric mills that most fashion brands never have. Our material brief is a living document. Every fabric we use must pass four tests: OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification, verified colorfastness at 60°C, elastic retention after 200 wash cycles, and documented supply chain traceability to the fiber source. If a fabric fails any one of these, it doesn't enter our range.
