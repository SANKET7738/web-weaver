Why Levante

A Different Kind of Dinner

Three principles guide everything we put on the table.

01

Seasonal & Sourced

Our mezze changes with the season. We work with regional suppliers to bring the freshest ingredients from farm and sea to your table.

02

Built for Sharing

Every plate is designed to be passed, tasted, and discussed. The more people at the table, the better the meal.

03

Rooted in Tradition

Our recipes draw from Lebanese, Turkish, Greek, and Israeli culinary traditions — interpreted with care and precision.

Our Story

Born from a Table in Beirut

Levante began as a memory — a long afternoon, a crowded table, and fifteen plates that never seemed to run out.

Our founders carried that experience back across the Mediterranean and spent two years perfecting the menu you'll find here today. Levante isn't just a restaurant. It's a format for gathering.
