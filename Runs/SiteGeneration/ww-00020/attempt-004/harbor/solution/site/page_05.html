Good to Know

Questions We Hear Often

FAQ · Levante

Do you cater to dietary requirements?

Yes. Our menu is clearly labeled for vegan (VG), vegetarian (V), and gluten-free (GF) options. Please inform your server of any allergies when you arrive.

How does mezze ordering work?

We recommend 3–4 plates per person as a guide, but there are no rules. Order as you go — our kitchen sends plates as they're ready.

Is there a minimum spend for private events?

Yes. Private dining room bookings have a minimum food spend of £600. Full venue buyouts are quoted on request.

Do you take walk-ins?

Always, subject to availability. For groups of 6 or more, we strongly recommend a reservation.
