The Beginning

An Afternoon That Refused to End

In 2019, co-founders Nadia Khalil and Marco Esposito sat down for what was supposed to be a quick lunch at a family-run mezze house in Beirut's Gemmayzeh neighborhood. Four hours later, they were still at the table.

The food had long since been cleared, but the conversation — and the feeling — refused to leave. They came home with notebooks full of recipes, supplier contacts, and a single shared conviction: that the mezze format, done right, could change how people eat together in any city in the world. Levante opened its first location in 2021.

Our Philosophy

How We Think About Food

Four commitments that shape everything from the supplier relationships to the plate presentation.

01

Provenance First

We know the name of every farm, fishery, and creamery we work with. If we can't trace it, we don't serve it.

02

Seasonal Without Exception

The menu shifts quarterly. Some favorites return. Others make way for what the season demands.

03

Hospitality as Practice

In the eastern Mediterranean, hospitality is not a service standard — it is a cultural obligation. We hold that standard.

04

The Long Lunch Principle

We design the experience so that no one feels rushed. The table is yours for the evening.
