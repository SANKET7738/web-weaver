Our Story

Born in a Garage.
Built for the World.

BoltWorks started with five kids, a pile of spare parts, and an unstoppable idea.

What began as an informal weekend tinkering group in 2016 has grown into one of the region's most celebrated youth robotics clubs — a place where every student, regardless of background or experience, gets a seat at the build table.

Mission & Values

We Build More
Than Robots

Our mission is to spark a lifelong love of engineering in every student who walks through our doors.

At BoltWorks, we believe the skills learned on the build floor — problem-solving, collaboration, resilience, and creative thinking — are the same skills that change the world. We don't just teach robotics. We build the next generation of engineers, innovators, and leaders.

Value 01

Curiosity First

We celebrate questions over answers and reward experimentation, even when the robot falls over.

Value 02

Every Builder Belongs

We actively recruit students from underrepresented communities and provide need-based scholarship access to all programs.

Value 03

Real-World Rigor

Our curriculum is designed with input from working engineers to ensure students learn skills that matter beyond the competition floor.

Value 04

Compete with Integrity

We train champions who win with grace, lose with dignity, and always help a rival team fix their robot before a match.
