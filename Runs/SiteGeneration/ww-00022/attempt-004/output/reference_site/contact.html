Quick Answers

Things People Usually Ask Us First

01

Does my child need prior experience to join?

Absolutely not. Most of our members start with zero robotics experience. Our Sparks and Voltage divisions are designed specifically for complete beginners.

+
02

What does membership cost?

Seasonal membership fees range from $150 to $350 depending on division. Need-based scholarships are available and cover up to 100% of fees. No student is turned away for financial reasons.

+
03

How do I enroll my child?

Submit an enrollment inquiry through the contact form above. Our team will schedule a free orientation session where your child can meet coaches and try a build activity before committing.

+
04

Can businesses or individuals sponsor BoltWorks?

Yes! Sponsorships start at $500 and include recognition on our competition robot, website, and event materials. Contact partners@boltworks.club for a sponsorship prospectus.

+
05

Do you partner with schools?

We have formal partnerships with six schools in the region and offer in-school demo programs and teacher training. Contact Tom Callahan at outreach@boltworks.club to explore a partnership.
