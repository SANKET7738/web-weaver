BoltWorks members don't just build competition robots — they push the boundaries of what young engineers can achieve.

From autonomous navigation to computer vision and soft robotics, our research projects have been presented at regional science fairs, university symposia, and national STEM conferences. This is where curiosity becomes contribution.

Active Projects

What We're Building Right Now

Each project is student-led, mentor-supported, and aimed at solving a real engineering challenge.

Project ARIA — Autonomous Robot Intelligence Architecture

Active Apex + Voltage Lead: Priya Nair

A Voltage and Apex team collaboration developing a modular autonomous decision-making system using ROS2 and a custom sensor fusion pipeline. Goal: sub-3-second field mapping in FRC-scale environments.

SoftBot — Pneumatic Soft Robotics Gripper

Active Innovation Lab Lead: Marcus Obi

An Innovation Lab project exploring soft robotic actuators made from silicone molds and pneumatic control. The team is testing grip reliability on irregular game pieces.

VisionTrack — Real-Time Object Detection for FRC

Active Apex Lead: Aisha Mensah

A software team project implementing a YOLOv8 model trained on competition game pieces to enable real-time targeting and autonomous scoring. Achieved 94% detection accuracy in field tests.

EcoBot — Sustainable Materials in Robot Construction

Active Innovation Lab + Outreach Lead: Tom Callahan

An outreach-driven research project investigating the use of recycled and bio-based materials in robot chassis design. Partnered with a local university materials science lab.

DriveMetrics — Drivetrain Performance Analytics Dashboard

Completed Apex Lead: Carlos Rivera

A data engineering project that logs motor telemetry during practice matches and visualizes performance trends to guide mechanical tuning decisions.
