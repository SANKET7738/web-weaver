BoltWorks is organized into specialized departments so every member can go deep on what they love most.

Whether you're drawn to the precision of mechanical design, the logic of code, the strategy of competition, or the art of communicating science — there's a department built for you.

DEPARTMENTS
Our Departments

Six Tracks. One Team.

Each department runs its own projects, workshops, and competitions — all feeding into the same championship robot.

Mechanical Engineering

Design and build the physical systems of the robot — chassis, drivetrain, manipulators, and structural components. CAD, prototyping, and fabrication are core skills.

Age 12–18 Beginner → Adv

Software & Controls

Write the code that makes the robot move, sense, and decide. Students work in Python, Java, and C++ using real robotics frameworks like WPILib and ROS.

Age 11–18 Beginner → Adv

Electrical & Wiring

Design the power systems, wire the motors and sensors, and ensure the robot's electronics are safe, reliable, and competition-ready.

Age 13–18 Intermediate+

Competition Strategy

Analyze opponents, plan match strategies, scout other teams, and make real-time tactical decisions during tournament play.

Age 10–18 All levels

Outreach & Communications

Tell the BoltWorks story to the world. Manage social media, write grant applications, organize community demos, and run our school ambassador program.

Age 10–18 All levels

Innovation Lab

A free-build sandbox for members who want to pursue independent robotics projects, enter external hackathons, or prototype ideas beyond the competition robot.

Age 8–18 All levels

Division Guide

Which Division Is
Right for You?

BoltWorks runs three age-based divisions, each with its own competition league and curriculum depth.

Sparks

Sparks Division

8–11

Introduction to robotics through LEGO Mindstorms and VEX IQ. Focus is on fun, exploration, and learning the basics of sensors and programming.

FormatWeekly 2-hour sessions
CompetitionJunior Robotics League
Voltage

Voltage Division

12–14

Intermediate builders working with VEX EDR and custom electronics. Teams design full robots for regional VEX competitions and internal BoltWorks tournaments.

Format2 sessions/week + weekend builds
CompetitionVEX Regional Circuit
Apex

Apex Division

15–18

The flagship program. Teams build full-scale competition robots for FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC). The most demanding and rewarding track BoltWorks offers.

Format3 sessions/week + intensive build
CompetitionFIRST Robotics (FRC)
Open Lab

Open Lab

All

Drop-in sessions for independent projects, skill workshops, and mentored free builds. No competition pressure — just making things.

FormatSaturday mornings
CompetitionNone — open exploration
