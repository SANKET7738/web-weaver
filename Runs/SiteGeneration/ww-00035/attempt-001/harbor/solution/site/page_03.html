How We Use Your Support
68% Animal Care 18% Operations 14% Outreach

Published annually each January. Full report available on request.

Every Dollar Accounted For.

Our operating budget is community-funded and publicly reported.

Approximately 68% of all funds received go directly to animal care — veterinary services, food, housing, and behavioral support. 18% supports shelter operations and staffing. 14% is invested in outreach, adoption campaigns, and community education. We publish a full annual report each January and make it available to anyone who asks.
