How Adoption Works

A Process Built Around Lasting Matches

We take adoption seriously because the animals depend on it.

01

Application

02

Meet & Greet

03

Review

04

Finalization

Our adoption process begins with a brief application form, followed by a meet-and-greet at the shelter. Our team reviews each application to ensure the match is right for both the animal and the household. Most adoptions are finalized within five to seven business days. We remain available for post-adoption support at no cost.
