Community & Partnerships

We Don't Do This Alone.

Haven & Paw works alongside veterinary practices, civic organizations, and local businesses who share our commitment to animal welfare.

Our Partners

Organizations That Stand With Us

Veterinary Partner

Riverside Veterinary Clinic

Provides discounted veterinary services and emergency medical care for all animals entering our facility.

Civic Agency

City Animal Control Division

Our primary municipal partner for intake coordination, emergency response, and stray animal transfer.

Corporate Sponsor

Greenfield Pet Supply Co.

Donates food, bedding, and supplies monthly, covering approximately 30% of our annual supply costs.

Funding Partner

Northside Community Foundation

Provides annual grant funding that supports our low-income adoption assistance program.

Network Affiliate

Regional SPCA Network

Coordinates inter-shelter animal transfers to reduce overcrowding and expand placement opportunities across the region.

Community Partner

Westbrook High School

Sends student volunteers each semester through their community service program, contributing hundreds of hours annually.

Become a Partner

Your Organization Can Make a Lasting Difference

We welcome partnerships with veterinary practices, civic organizations, foundations, and businesses aligned with our mission.

Whether you can offer in-kind donations, grant funding, volunteer programs, or professional services, there is a meaningful role for your organization at Haven & Paw. We formalize all partnerships with a simple agreement and publish partner names in our annual report.
