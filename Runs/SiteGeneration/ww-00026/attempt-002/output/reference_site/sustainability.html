Our Four Pillars

How we think about doing better

Every sustainability decision at Lumi & Bloom runs through these four lenses.

Clean Formulation

We source only responsibly harvested, sustainably farmed botanical ingredients. No petrochemical derivatives. Full supply chain transparency.

Circular Packaging

We use post-consumer recycled plastics, glass, and aluminum. Our refill program lets customers return empties for a discount on their next order.

Climate Responsibility

All operations and shipping are carbon offset. We publish our annual carbon footprint report so you can hold us accountable.

Community Giving

1% of every sale goes to environmental and women's wellness nonprofits selected by our customer community each year.

The Roadmap

Where we've been and where we're going

We share this publicly because accountability is part of the commitment.

2021

Recycled Packaging Introduced

Switched to post-consumer recycled materials for all bottles and jars, eliminating virgin plastic from 60% of the range.

Complete

Partnered with a certified reforestation program to offset 100% of shipping emissions on every order.

Complete

Launched a refill program for our top three products, reducing single-use packaging by an estimated 18,000 units annually.
