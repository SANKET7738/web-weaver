What's In Every Formula

Clean by design, not by claim

Every Lumi & Bloom product is held to the same non-negotiable ingredient standards.

No Parabens

We never use paraben-based preservatives. Our formulas use naturally derived alternatives.

No Synthetic Dyes

All color comes from natural botanical pigments or the ingredients themselves.

No Sulfates

Gentle, sulfate-free cleansing agents only — safe for even the most sensitive skin.

Dermatologist Reviewed

Every formula is reviewed by an independent dermatologist before it reaches our shelves.
