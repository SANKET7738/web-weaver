How It Started

A Kitchen Table, a Problem, and a Promise

Lumi & Bloom was founded in 2019 by two friends who couldn't find a body care brand that checked every box: genuinely clean formulas, ingredients they could pronounce, sustainable packaging, and a price point that didn't feel like a luxury tax. So they made their own. Starting with a small batch of whipped shea butter in a shared apartment kitchen, they spent two years testing, refining, and listening to their community. Today, Lumi & Bloom ships to customers across the country — but the founding promise hasn't changed: every product should make you feel taken care of, inside and out.

What We Stand For

Our Values Aren't a Marketing Slide — They're Our Ingredient List

Every decision we make — from formula to packaging to partnerships — runs through these four commitments.

Radically Transparent

Full ingredient lists, sourcing notes, and formulation rationale published for every product. No fine print, no mystery.

Genuinely Clean

No parabens, sulfates, synthetic dyes, or fragrance shortcuts. Every ingredient earns its place in the formula.

Sustainably Packaged

Recycled and recyclable materials across our full range. We're on a path to 100% plastic-free by 2026.

Community First

We co-develop products with our customer community. Your skin feedback shapes our next launch.

Our Journey

From First Batch to Beloved Brand

  1. 2019

    — The First Formula

    Lavender Dream Butter is made in a home kitchen and shared with 50 friends. The feedback is unanimous: make more.

  2. 2020

    — First 1,000 Customers

    Lumi & Bloom launches online with four products and sells out within three weeks of its first campaign.

  3. 2021

    — Clean Beauty Certified

    We receive third-party clean beauty certification and introduce our first refillable packaging option.

  4. 2022

    — Community Co-Creation

    The Citrus Burst Sugar Scrub is developed entirely based on community votes and ingredient suggestions.

  5. 2023

    — 50,000 Customers & Counting

    We cross 50,000 happy customers and launch our sustainability pledge: plastic-free packaging by 2026.
