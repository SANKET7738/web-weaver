What We Do

From Raw Data to Realized Space

Our services span the full lifecycle of a public space project — from the first survey to the final paving stone.

Core Services

A Full-Spectrum Design Practice

Phase 01

Urban Research & Diagnostics

We begin every engagement with a rigorous site analysis: pedestrian counts, behavioral observation, environmental sensing, and stakeholder interviews to establish a quantified baseline.

01
Phase 02

Community Engagement & Data Synthesis

Structured participatory processes — surveys, workshops, and co-design sessions — generate qualitative data that is coded, weighted, and integrated alongside quantitative findings.

02
Phase 03

Evidence-Based Concept Design

Design proposals are generated directly from research outputs. Every spatial decision is traceable to a data point, ensuring defensible, community-aligned outcomes.

03
Phase 04

Environmental Performance Analysis

We model microclimate, solar access, acoustic conditions, and stormwater performance to optimize comfort, resilience, and ecological value across all seasons.

04
Phase 05

Implementation Documentation

Detailed construction documentation, material specifications, and phasing strategies ensure design intent is preserved through procurement and build.

05
Phase 06

Post-Occupancy Evaluation

We return after opening to measure real-world performance against projected metrics, generating a feedback loop that sharpens every future project.
