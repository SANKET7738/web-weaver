Our Story

Founded on a Frustration with Guesswork

Axiom Public Lab was built on the belief that public space design deserved the same analytical rigor applied to infrastructure engineering.

In 2009, Dr. Nadia Osei watched a newly opened plaza in Rotterdam sit almost entirely empty within six months of opening — despite winning a design award. The problem wasn't aesthetics. It was that nobody had measured how people actually moved through that part of the city before designing it. Axiom Public Lab was founded the following year with a single operating principle: every design decision must be traceable to evidence. Fifteen years later, that principle drives every project, every hire, and every method we develop.
