About Pixelwave

We Cover the Future So You Don't Get Left Behind

Pixelwave is an independent technology publication built for readers who want to understand what's actually happening — not just what went viral.

Our Story

Born in the Browser, Built for the Next Era

Pixelwave launched in 2021 because we were tired of technology journalism that either fawned over founders or reduced every breakthrough to a bullet-point explainer. The future is complicated, contested, and genuinely exciting — and it deserved a publication willing to sit with that complexity.

We are a small, independent team of journalists, technologists, and obsessives who believe that the best technology writing is also great writing. We do not accept sponsored content. We do not write press releases in disguise. Every story we publish has been reported, argued over, edited, and re-edited until it earns its place.

"The future is buffering — and we are here to help you read the signal through the noise."

Our editorial philosophy is simple: respect the reader's intelligence, take the technology seriously, and never mistake hype for substance. The future is buffering — and we are here to help you read the signal through the noise.
