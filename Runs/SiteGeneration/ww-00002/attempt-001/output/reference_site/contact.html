Common Questions

Before you hit send.

Do I need a credit card to start a trial?

No. The 14-day trial is fully featured and requires only a work email address. You will not be charged unless you explicitly upgrade.

+

How long does an enterprise evaluation take?

Most enterprise evaluations run 3–4 weeks from first demo to signed contract. We provide a dedicated sandbox environment and a security package on day one.

+

Can I get your SOC 2 report before signing anything?

Yes. We provide our SOC 2 Type II executive summary under a standard mutual NDA. Full report access is included in the enterprise evaluation package.

+

Do you offer volume discounts for high-event-volume customers?

Yes. Enterprise pricing is based on monthly event volume with tiered discounts. Our solutions engineers will model pricing against your current or projected usage.

+

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You have 30 days after cancellation to export all your data via our API or dashboard. After 30 days, all data is permanently and verifiably deleted. You receive a deletion certificate.
