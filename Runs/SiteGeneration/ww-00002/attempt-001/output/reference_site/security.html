Infrastructure Security

Defense in depth, from the edge to the archive.

TLS 1.3 Everywhere

All data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.3. We enforce HSTS and reject connections from clients that cannot support modern cipher suites.

AES-256 At Rest

All stored data is encrypted with AES-256. Encryption keys are managed by a dedicated KMS with automatic 90-day rotation.

Zero-Trust Network Architecture

Internal services communicate over mTLS. No service has implicit trust. Every request is authenticated and authorized independently.

Immutable Audit Logs

All system and user actions are written to an append-only, cryptographically signed audit log. Logs cannot be modified or deleted by any user, including admins.

Annual Penetration Testing

We contract an independent third-party firm for full-scope penetration testing every year. Executive summaries are available to enterprise customers under NDA.

Vulnerability Disclosure Program

Our responsible disclosure program is open to all researchers. Critical findings are triaged within 24 hours and patched within 72 hours.

Our Privacy Philosophy

We built Nullpoint on a simple premise: the best way to protect user data is to never collect it.

Most analytics companies approach privacy as a compliance problem — a set of rules to satisfy while collecting as much data as possible. We approached it as a design constraint. If a feature requires personal data, we ask whether there is a privacy-preserving alternative that delivers equivalent insight. In most cases, there is. Differential privacy, k-anonymity thresholds, and aggregate-only APIs are not afterthoughts at Nullpoint — they are the foundation. The result is a platform where your users' privacy is protected not by policy, but by architecture.

You cannot leak what you never collected.
