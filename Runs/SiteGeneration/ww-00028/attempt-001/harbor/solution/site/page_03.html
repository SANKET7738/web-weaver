Core Services

Plans Designed for Every Stage of Life

Last Will & Testament

A legally binding document that specifies how your assets will be distributed, names guardians for minor children, and appoints an executor to carry out your wishes. Ideal for individuals and families at any asset level.

Ideal for · Individuals, parents with minor children

Revocable Living Trust

A flexible trust that holds your assets during your lifetime and transfers them to beneficiaries without probate. Maintains privacy and allows you to retain full control while you are living.

Ideal for · Homeowners, blended families

Durable Power of Attorney

Designates a trusted agent to manage your financial affairs — paying bills, managing investments, filing taxes — if you become unable to do so yourself.

Ideal for · All adults, especially with aging parents

Healthcare Directive & Living Will

Documents your medical treatment preferences and designates a healthcare proxy to make decisions on your behalf if you are incapacitated.

Ideal for · All adults 18 and older

Special Needs Trust

Provides for a family member with disabilities without disqualifying them from government benefits such as Medicaid or SSI. Carefully structured to supplement, not replace, public assistance.

Ideal for · Families with a disabled dependent

Business Succession Planning

Ensures your business continues smoothly after your retirement, incapacity, or death. Includes buy-sell agreements, ownership transfer strategies, and key-person planning.

Ideal for · Business owners, entrepreneurs
