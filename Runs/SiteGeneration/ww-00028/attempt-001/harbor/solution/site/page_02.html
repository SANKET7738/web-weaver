Our Story

Built on the Belief That Every Family Deserves a Plan

Hearthstone Law was founded on the conviction that estate planning is one of the most profound acts of love a person can undertake.

Why We Do This Work

A Practice Born from Personal Experience

Make thoughtful, thorough estate planning accessible to every family.

Founding attorney Eleanor Marsh began her career at a large regional firm, where she watched families navigate the chaos of losing a loved one without a plan in place. She saw estates tied up in probate for years, sibling relationships fractured over ambiguous wills, and grieving spouses left without access to basic funds. In 2009, she opened Hearthstone Law with a singular mission: to make thoughtful, thorough estate planning accessible to every family — not just the wealthy few. Today, the firm has helped over 1,400 families across the region put plans in place they can trust.
