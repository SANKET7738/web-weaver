Common Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Is there a joining fee?

Nope. No joining fee, no setup fee, no surprises. You pay your monthly rate and that's it.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?

Yes. You can freeze your membership for up to 2 months per year and cancel with 30 days' notice — no questions asked.

Do programs cost extra?

Structured programs like Barbell Basics and Pop Powerlifting are included in all memberships. 1-on-1 coaching is available as an add-on or included in the Elite Lifter plan.

What does the free session include?

A 45-minute coached session, a tour of the gym, and a quick goal-setting chat with one of our coaches.
