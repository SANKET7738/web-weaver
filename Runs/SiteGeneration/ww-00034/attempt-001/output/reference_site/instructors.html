How We Hire

Getting On This Roster Is Not Easy.

Every BRUTALK instructor application goes through a four-stage vetting process: a language proficiency assessment at C2 or native level, a live teaching demonstration with a panel of senior instructors, a curriculum design exercise reviewed by our academic team, and a six-week probationary cohort with student feedback scoring. We reject approximately 80% of applicants. The 20% who make it through are the ones your fluency depends on. We don't apologize for the bar being high.

The Vetting Pipeline

Four Stages. No Shortcuts.

1

Stage 1 — Proficiency Assessment

All applicants complete a standardized C2-level written and spoken proficiency test in their target language. Native speakers are not exempt.

2

Stage 2 — Live Teaching Demo

A 30-minute live class delivered to a panel of three senior BRUTALK instructors acting as students. Evaluated on clarity, pacing, error correction, and engagement.

3

Stage 3 — Curriculum Design Exercise

Applicants design a 4-week A2 lesson plan aligned to CEFR descriptors. Reviewed by our academic advisory board for rigor and originality.

4

Stage 4 — Probationary Cohort

Accepted applicants run one live cohort under observation. Student feedback is collected, scored, and reviewed. Instructors who pass are offered a permanent contract.
