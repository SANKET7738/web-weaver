Before You Commit

The Questions That Are Stopping You. Answered.

Q:Is the first class really free?
A:Yes. Pick any available session, book a seat, show up. No card required. If it's not for you, we'd rather know now.
Q:What if I need to pause my plan?
A:You can pause for up to 30 days once per billing cycle. More than that and you'll need to cancel and re-enroll. We don't do indefinite holds.
Q:Can I upgrade or downgrade mid-month?
A:Yes. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Prorated credits are applied automatically.
Q:Are there discounts for students or educators?
A:We offer a 20% discount for verified full-time students and a 15% discount for language educators. Apply via the account settings after sign-up.
Q:What if my instructor leaves the platform?
A:You'll be placed in a new cohort with a comparable instructor within 48 hours. No sessions missed, no credit lost.
