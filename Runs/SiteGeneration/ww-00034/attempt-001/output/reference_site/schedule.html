Why the Schedule Is Built This Way

Frequency Is the Mechanism. Not Motivation.

Language acquisition research is unambiguous: distributed practice beats marathon sessions. BRUTALK's schedule is built around two 90-minute live sessions per week — enough frequency to maintain active recall, short enough to demand full presence every time.

We don't offer daily classes because daily classes produce fatigue, not fluency. We don't offer weekly classes because one session a week produces forgetting, not progress.

Two sessions. Ninety minutes each. Every week. That's the dose.
