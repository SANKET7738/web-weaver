Heat Guide

Not Sure Where to Start?

Here's how our sauces stack up on the Lava Mouth Heat Scale.

🔥 Mild
Serrano Verde

Flavor-forward, gentle warmth. Perfect for everyday use and heat newcomers.

🔥🔥 Medium
Smoky Diablo

Noticeable heat that builds slowly. Great for those who want more than a whisper.

🔥🔥🔥 Hot
Mango Inferno · Pineapple Fury

A genuine burn with serious flavor complexity. The sweet spot for most heat lovers.

🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 Extra Hot
Ghost Town

For experienced palates only. Intense, layered, and deeply satisfying.

🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥+ Extreme
Carolina Reaper's Edge

You've been warned. This is the frontier. Bring water. Bring a friend.
