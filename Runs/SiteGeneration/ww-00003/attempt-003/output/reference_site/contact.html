How quickly will I hear back?
We respond to all consultation requests within one business day. Most clients hear back the same afternoon.
Is the consultation really free?
Yes — always. The 15-minute call is designed to help us understand your needs and answer your questions, with no obligation to book.
What if I'm not sure I'm ready for therapy?
That's exactly what the consultation is for. Many of our clients use it just to ask questions and get a feel for the process before committing.
Do you have a cancellation policy?
We ask for 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations may incur a fee, which your therapist will explain at intake.
Can I request a specific therapist?
Absolutely. If you've reviewed our practitioners page and have a preference, let us know in your message and we'll do our best to accommodate.
