In-Person vs. Telehealth

Both paths lead to real progress.

Here's how to decide what works best for you.

In-Person Therapy

Best for clients who value face-to-face connection, a dedicated physical space to decompress, and minimal screen time during sessions.

Availability Mon–Sat
Location Our downtown office suite

Telehealth Therapy

Best for clients with busy schedules, mobility limitations, or who simply feel more comfortable opening up from their own space.

Availability Mon–Sun
Location Anywhere with a secure connection
