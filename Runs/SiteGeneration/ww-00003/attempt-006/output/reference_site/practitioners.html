Our Philosophy

Therapy is a relationship, not a transaction.

Clearmind Therapy was founded on a simple belief: that the quality of the relationship between therapist and client is the single most powerful predictor of healing. We don't rush intake. We don't overload caseloads. We hire practitioners who believe in their work and take the time to know each person they sit with. Every therapist on our team has been chosen not only for their credentials, but for their capacity to be genuinely present. That's the Clearmind difference.

The relationship is the therapy.
