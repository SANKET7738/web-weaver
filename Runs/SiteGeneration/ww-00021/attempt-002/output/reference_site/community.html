Regular Events

Something On Every Week.

Pick your night, grab a friend (or come solo), and show up ready to play.

Open Play Nights

Every Tuesday and Thursday evening, the floor is open for drop-in play. No booking required. Meet new people, try new games.

Tuesdays & Thursdays Table fee applies

Beginner Sundays

New to board games? Our Game Guides run structured introductory sessions every Sunday afternoon. Perfect for curious newcomers.

Every Sunday, 2–5pm Free with booking

Monthly Tournament

Competitive players gather on the last Saturday of every month for a single-game tournament. Prizes, glory, and bragging rights.

Last Saturday monthly £5 entry fee

Game of the Month Club

Each month we spotlight one game. Buy it, play it, discuss it. A book club for board game lovers.

Monthly Free to join

Private Group Sessions

Organise your own gaming group with a reserved table, a dedicated Game Guide, and a curated game selection.

By arrangement Group rates

Kids' Game Morning

Saturday mornings are for families. Age-appropriate games, patient guides, and a relaxed pace.

Saturdays, 10am–12pm Reduced fee
