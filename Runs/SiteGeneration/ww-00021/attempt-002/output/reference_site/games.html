Staff Picks

Games Our Team Can't Stop Recommending.

Ask any of us and we'll talk for twenty minutes. Here are a few obsessions worth knowing about.

Wingspan

A gorgeous engine-builder about birds. Surprisingly competitive, endlessly replayable. Suitable for 1–5 players.

1–5 players 40–70 min Medium

Codenames

The ultimate word-association party game. Two teams, one spy chief per side, and a board full of secrets.

2–8+ players 15–30 min Easy

Gloomhaven: Jaws of the Lion

A streamlined entry into the legendary dungeon-crawl epic. Perfect for groups who want a campaign experience.

1–4 players 60–120 min Hard

Azul

Abstract tile-drafting with stunning components. Simple rules, deep tactics, and beautiful to look at.

2–4 players 30–45 min Easy-Medium
Our Game Guides

Never Stare at a Rulebook Alone Again.

Every table at Rollout has access to a trained Game Guide — a staff member who lives and breathes tabletop. Tell them your group size, how long you want to play, and whether you're in the mood for laughs or brain-burning strategy. They'll bring you the perfect game, teach you the rules in under five minutes, and check in throughout your session. No intimidation. No confusion. Just play.
