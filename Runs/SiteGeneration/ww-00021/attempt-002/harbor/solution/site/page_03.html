Our Story

We Built the Café We Always Wished Existed.

Rollout started at a kitchen table. Two friends, a copy of Pandemic, and a shared frustration that there was nowhere in the city to play great games with great people. So we found a space, filled it with shelves, trained a team of game obsessives, and opened the doors. That was three years ago. Since then, thousands of players have sat across from each other, laughed, argued, strategised, and left with new friends. We're not a bar with a few games on a shelf. We're a place built entirely around the joy of play.

How We Got Here

Three Years of Game Nights.

2022

Year One: We Open

Rollout opens with 120 games, 8 tables, and more excitement than we knew what to do with. We sell out every weekend for the first two months.

Early 2023

The Library Grows

We hit 300 games and launch the Game Guide programme. Guest satisfaction scores jump immediately.

Mid 2023

Private Rooms Launch

We expand into the unit next door, adding three private event rooms. Corporate bookings take off.

Late 2023

The Online Shop Opens

Guests kept asking to buy their favourite games. We listened. The shop launches with 200 titles available to order.

2024

Community Events Begin

Weekly open-play nights, monthly tournaments, and a beginner series bring in a new wave of regulars.

Today

500 Games & Counting

The library crosses 500 titles. We're still adding new releases every month.
