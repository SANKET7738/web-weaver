Inkwell Jazz Festival 2025
Forty artists. Three stages. Zero bad seats.

From jazz legends to the names you'll be telling your friends about next year.

Headliners

The names that draw the crowd.

Marlena Voss Quartet

Friday · Main Stage · 9:00 PM

The voice that redefined contemporary jazz. Marlena's set promises material from her landmark album "Indigo Hours" alongside new arrangements premiered exclusively at Inkwell.

The Coltrane Collective

Saturday · Main Stage · 9:30 PM

A rotating ensemble of twelve musicians paying living tribute to the free jazz tradition. No two sets are the same. Expect the unexpected.

Esperanza Blue

Sunday · Main Stage · 8:45 PM

Closing the festival with her signature blend of Afro-Cuban jazz and soul, Esperanza Blue brings the curtain down on Inkwell in the most joyful way imaginable.

Supporting Acts & Rising Voices

The artists you'll discover this weekend.

Theo Adkins Trio

— Friday · 7:00 PM Inkwell Tent

Modal jazz with a modern edge. Theo's piano work has been called "the most interesting thing happening in jazz right now."

Sable & the Horns

— Saturday · 2:00 PM Sketch Stage

A seven-piece brass ensemble rooted in New Orleans tradition. Daytime perfection.

Nora Kimani

— Sunday · 6:30 PM Inkwell Tent

Vocalist and composer whose debut EP sold out on vinyl in 48 hours. Her Inkwell set is her largest to date.

Riverside Collective

— Saturday · 11:00 AM Sketch Stage

Local heroes and Inkwell originals. The Collective has opened the festival every year since its founding.
