The Inkwell Experience

Three days drawn from the best of jazz culture.

settle in, take your time
What’s On

More than a concert. A whole world.

The Main Stage

Our flagship open-air stage hosts headline acts each evening as the sun sets over Riverside Meadows. Capacity for 8,000 — intimate enough to feel the music.

The Inkwell Tent

A candlelit marquee for late-night sets, experimental jazz, and surprise collaborations. Capacity is deliberately small. Arrive early.

The Sketch Stage

Our daytime acoustic stage celebrates emerging artists and local talent. Every set is free to attend with a festival wristband.

Jazz Workshops

Sit in on masterclasses led by performing artists. Improvisation, composition, and music history sessions run throughout the weekend.

The Food Village

Over 30 independent food vendors, craft breweries, and specialty coffee roasters — all curated to match the handmade spirit of the festival.

The Illustration Market

A dedicated marketplace of independent artists, printmakers, and record sellers. Take home something hand-drawn as a memory of the weekend.

The Feeling

You’ll smell the cedar smoke before you hear the first note.

Inkwell is built for lingering. For the conversation that starts between sets. For the sketch you make on a paper cup. For the musician who plays an extra song because the crowd won’t let them leave. We design every corner of the festival grounds the way a good album is sequenced — so that each moment leads naturally into the next, and the whole thing feels inevitable by the time it’s over.
