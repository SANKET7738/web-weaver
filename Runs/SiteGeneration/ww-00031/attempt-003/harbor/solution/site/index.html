Our Story

Born from a love letter to jazz.

Inkwell started as a sketch on a napkin. Eight years later, it’s the most anticipated jazz weekend of the summer.

We didn’t set out to build a festival. We set out to build a feeling — the kind you get when a saxophonist closes their eyes and the whole crowd holds its breath. Every stage, every set, every hand-lettered sign at Inkwell is made with that feeling in mind. Come as a stranger. Leave humming something you’ve never heard before.
