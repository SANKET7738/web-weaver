Key Locations

Everything you need to know about where to go.

Entrance

Main Entrance & Wristband Exchange

Located on Meadow Lane, Gate A. Open from 10:00 AM each day. Bring your ticket QR code or printed confirmation for wristband collection.

Stages

Main Stage

South-east corner of the meadow. Capacity 8,000. Gates open 90 minutes before each headline set. Accessible viewing platform available on the west side.

Stages

The Inkwell Tent

North of the Main Stage, 5-minute walk. Capacity 600. No reserved seating — arrive early for late-night sets.

Food & Drink

Food Village & Bars

Central meadow, between the Sketch Stage and the Illustration Market. Open from 11:00 AM daily. Cashless payment only — top up your festival card at any info point.

Activities

Workshop & Talk Tents

North-west corner, adjacent to the Sketch Stage. Sessions run 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM each day. No booking required — just walk in.

Facilities

Accessibility & First Aid

First Aid is located near Gate A and behind the Main Stage. Accessible entrances, viewing platforms, and quiet zones are marked in blue on the festival map.

Before You Arrive

Questions we've already answered for you.

Sealed water bottles and small snacks are welcome. Glass containers, alcohol, and large cool boxes are not permitted on the festival grounds.

Yes — Meadow Car Park is a 10-minute walk from Gate A. We strongly encourage the use of our shuttle bus service from Riverside Station, which runs every 20 minutes from 9:00 AM.

Inkwell is fully accessible. Accessible viewing platforms are available at all three stages, and accessible toilets are located throughout the site. Contact us in advance to arrange any additional support.

Inkwell is an all-weather festival. The Inkwell Tent and Workshop Tents provide covered areas. We recommend layers and waterproofs — the music plays regardless.
