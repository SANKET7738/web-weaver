Everything You're Wondering About the $99 Hold

Is the $99 really refundable?

Yes. 100%. If you decide not to buy for any reason — or no reason at all — we refund your $99 within 1 business day. No questions, no fees, no hassle.

How long does the hold last?

Your hold keeps the vehicle off the market for 5 business days. Need more time? Contact your advisor and we'll work with you.

Can I still negotiate the price after reserving?

The listed price is already market-validated and firm. What you see is what you pay — no negotiation theater.

What happens after I place the hold?

You'll get an email confirmation immediately. Your RevvLot advisor will contact you within 2 business hours to schedule inspection, arrange financing if needed, and confirm delivery or pickup.

Can I reserve more than one car?

You can place holds on up to 2 vehicles at a time. Each hold is independent and fully refundable.
